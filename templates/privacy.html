{{ shared.app_name() }} ("we", "us", "our") operates the {{ shared.app_name() }} website and service (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create an account, sign in, or use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information — your first name, last name, and email address. If you sign up with your email, we assign you a default avatar and email you a one-time code to confirm the address and to sign you in; we keep only a hashed copy of each code, and it expires after 10 minutes.
  • Information from Google — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL, and use them in place of what you entered. We never receive your Google password.
  • Sign-in and network information — your IP address each time you sign in and the last IP address you used the Service from. We look up the approximate city, region, and country of the IP address you sign in from using a geolocation database. Each sign-in (time, IP address, and location) is kept in a sign-in history for 30 days; your most recent sign-in is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Activity information — when you were last active, and a record of each day on which you used the Service while signed in. We use this to measure how the Service is used.
  • Billing information — if you buy a paid plan, payments are processed by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer identifier and your plan. Your card details are entered with and held by Stripe; they never reach our servers.
  • Content you submit — anything you post to the Service and your account settings.
  • Cookies — see the cookies section below.

Who can see it

Our administrators can see your account information, sign-in history, locations, and activity, and can look up which accounts have signed in from a given IP address. They may also keep private notes and flags on an account. We use this to support you and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse — for example, accounts that sign in from an IP address tied to a suspended account, or from [RESTRICTED COUNTRIES, if any], may be suspended automatically.

How we use information

  • To create and authenticate your account, including emailing you sign-in codes.
  • To process payments and manage your plan.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • To send you essential service messages (e.g. security notices).

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information with:

  • Service providers who run the Service on our behalf: [HOSTING PROVIDER] for hosting and our database, Google for sign-in (if you choose it), Stripe for payments, and [EMAIL PROVIDER] for sending email. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of any person.
  • Successors in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small set of strictly necessary cookies:

  • auth_token — a signed token that keeps you signed in for up to a year, or until you sign out. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure when the Service runs over HTTPS.
  • oauth_nonce — a random value set when you start signing in with Google and removed when you finish. It ties the sign-in to your browser to defend against cross-site request forgery.
